Why can't CheckLah read my ticket?

Good lighting and a flat, unfolded ticket make the biggest difference. If a number still looks wrong after scanning, use the review screen's edit option to correct it before checking — this is built in specifically because ticket text can sometimes be misread.

Does CheckLah need an account?

No. There's no sign-up or login. Your scan history and favorites are stored only on your device.

Is CheckLah affiliated with Singapore Pools?

No. CheckLah is developed independently and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Singapore Pools. It only checks tickets you already own against publicly published results.
