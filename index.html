CheckLah — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

CheckLah ("the App", "we", "our") is a utility app that scans and checks Singapore Pools 4D and Toto lottery tickets against historical draw results. This page explains what information the App accesses, what it does with it, and what it does not do.

Summary

CheckLah does not require you to create an account, does not collect personal information, does not track you across apps or websites, and does not contain advertising. Everything the App does with a scanned ticket happens on your own device.

Information we access, and why

  • Camera. Used only when you choose to scan a physical lottery ticket. Photos you take are processed on your device (text recognition happens locally) and are not uploaded to us or to any third party.
  • Photo library. Used only if you choose "Choose from Photos" to scan a ticket image you already have. Same as above — the image is read and processed locally, never uploaded.
  • Motion / shake gesture. Used only to detect a physical shake of your device, which triggers the "Lucky Numbers" feature. No motion data is stored or transmitted anywhere.

Data stored on your device

The App keeps a local, on-device database of:

  • Historical 4D and Toto draw results (bundled with the App and periodically refreshed — see "Network access" below).
  • Your own scan history, favorites/watchlist, and app preferences.

None of this is linked to your identity, and none of it is uploaded to us. If you delete the App, this data is deleted with it.

Network access

The App periodically downloads a small, publicly-hosted file containing recent official 4D/Toto draw results, so your results stay up to date without you doing anything. This is a one-way download of public lottery-result data — the App does not send any personal information, device identifiers, or usage data as part of this, or at any other time.

What we do not do

  • We do not require or support creating an account.
  • We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, or any other personal identifier.
  • We do not use analytics or advertising SDKs, and the App shows no ads.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or share any data, because we do not collect any to begin with.

Children's privacy

CheckLah is a general-audience utility tool and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age, because it does not collect personal information at all.

Not a betting or gambling service

CheckLah does not let you place bets, purchase tickets, or wager money of any kind. It only reads a ticket you already own (via your camera or photo library) and compares the numbers printed on it against publicly published Singapore Pools draw results. CheckLah is developed independently of Singapore Pools and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Singapore Pools.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this same page with a new "Last updated" date.
