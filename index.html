Privacy Policy

CheckLah

4D & Toto ticket checker — Singapore

EFFECTIVE 02 NOV 2026 VER 1.0
In short
  • CheckLah doesn't need an account or login — there's nothing to sign up for.
  • Your scanned tickets and favorite numbers stay on your device. We never see them, and they're never uploaded anywhere.
  • A ticket photo is read entirely on your device to extract the numbers — the photo itself is never sent to a server.
  • CheckLah shows ads through Google AdMob, which collects some advertising data to do that — details in Section 1.
  • You can clear your data anytime from the History screen, or by deleting the app.
01

Information we collect

Stored only on your device

CheckLah keeps the following directly on your phone, in the app's own local database — never on a server we operate:

  • Scanned ticket details you've checked (game type, numbers, stake, date, and result)
  • Favorite / watchlisted numbers
  • App settings and preferences

This data is removed when you delete the record (swipe-to-delete in History or Favorites) or when you uninstall CheckLah. If your device backs up to iCloud or a computer, that backup may include this local data as part of your device's normal backup process — that's managed by Apple's own iOS settings, not by CheckLah.

Camera and photos

When you grant camera access, CheckLah uses it to photograph a physical ticket. That photo is read on-device using Google's ML Kit text recognition to extract the printed numbers — the image is processed entirely on your phone and is never uploaded to any server, by us or anyone else. If you choose "Choose from Photos" instead, the same on-device processing applies to the single photo you select.

Advertising data (Google AdMob)

CheckLah shows ads through Google's AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to help keep the app free. To serve and measure those ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect device and advertising identifiers, an approximate location derived from your IP address, and information about how you interact with ads. This collection is governed by Google's own policies, not CheckLah's — see Google's Privacy Policy and how Google's advertising partners use data.

If you're in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, CheckLah shows a consent message the first time you open the app so you can choose whether to allow this, including a direct option to decline.

Draw results

CheckLah fetches publicly published 4D and Toto draw results from a server we host, so you see current results without needing an app update. This is a standard network request and doesn't include anything that identifies you beyond what's inherent to any internet connection, such as your IP address, used only to deliver the response.

02

How we use this information

We use it to let you scan and check tickets against draw results, and to show ads that help keep CheckLah free. We don't use your information for anything beyond what's described in this policy, and we don't sell it.

03

Sharing

We don't share your locally-stored ticket or favorites data with anyone — we have no access to it in the first place, since it lives only on your device. Advertising data is shared with Google and its advertising partners as described in Section 1, under their own privacy terms.

04

Your choices

  • Delete local data — swipe to delete any scanned ticket in History or any entry in Favorites, or uninstall CheckLah to remove all of it from your device.
  • Ad personalization — manage this through the in-app consent message (EEA/UK/Switzerland) or your device's own ad-tracking settings, under iOS Settings → Privacy & Security.
  • Camera and Photos access — change these anytime under iOS Settings → CheckLah.
05

Children's privacy

CheckLah isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from children.

06

International users

CheckLah is built around Singapore Pools' 4D and Toto results and is intended mainly for use in Singapore. If you use it from elsewhere, including the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, the practices and choices described in this policy still apply.

07

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as CheckLah changes. The effective date at the top reflects the latest version.
